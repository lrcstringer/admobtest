iMaliChat — account and data deletion request

You can delete your iMaliChat account at any time, either directly inside the app or by contacting us via email. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

Follow these steps in iMaliChat:
  1. Open iMaliChat on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile icon or go to Settings (bottom navigation → Profile tab → gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen.
  4. Tap Delete Account (shown in red).
  5. Read the confirmation prompt and tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately upon confirmation. You will be signed out and cannot recover the account.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to our privacy team:

Email: privacy@imalichat.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Include: the phone number linked to your account (in E.164 format, e.g. +27821234567)

Email Us to Delete Account

We will process your request within 5 business days and confirm deletion by reply email.

What data is deleted and what is retained

Data type Action on account deletion Retention period
Profile (name, username, photo, phone number, date of birth, gender, location) Permanently deleted Removed from active databases within 30 days
Messages and chat history Permanently deleted from our servers Server-side metadata purged within 30 days; local device copy removed when app is uninstalled
Token wallet balance Forfeited — unredeemed tokens are cancelled N/A (no monetary value)
Token transaction records Anonymised — your name and phone number are removed Retained for 7 years for financial compliance purposes
Survey and engagement responses Anonymised Retained in anonymised form for up to 12 months for analytics
Device binding and security logs Deleted Removed within 30 days
FCM push notification tokens Deleted immediately None
Important: Account deletion is irreversible. Your username, token balance, chat history, and earned rewards cannot be recovered after deletion. If you have tokens you wish to redeem, please do so before deleting your account.
