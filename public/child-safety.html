Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 2 April 2026

iMaliChat is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This page outlines our standards and the steps we take to protect minors.

1. Age Requirement

iMaliChat is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under the age of 18 to create an account. Account creation requires phone number verification via OTP; if we become aware that a user is under 18, we will suspend and delete their account immediately.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE

iMaliChat has an absolute prohibition on child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that sexually exploits, endangers, or grooms minors. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Images, videos, or text that sexually depicts or exploits minors.
  • Solicitation of minors for sexual purposes.
  • Grooming behaviour directed at minors.
  • Sharing, distributing, or linking to CSAM.

Any account found engaging in such behaviour will be permanently banned and reported to the relevant authorities, including the South African Police Service (SAPS) and the Film and Publication Board (FPB).

3. Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report content or behaviour that violates these standards through the following channels:

  • In-app reporting: Long-press any message or tap the menu on any profile to access the Report option.
  • Email: safety@imalichat.com — reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

All reports are treated confidentially. We do not disclose the identity of the person who submitted a report.

4. Content Moderation

  • All peer-to-peer messages are end-to-end encrypted; however, users can still report messages to our safety team.
  • Profile photos and publicly visible content are subject to review upon report.
  • Repeated violations result in permanent account termination.

5. Legal Compliance

iMaliChat complies with all applicable South African laws regarding child protection, including the:

  • Children's Act 38 of 2005
  • Films and Publications Act 65 of 1996 (as amended)
  • Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)
  • Cybercrimes Act 19 of 2020

Where required by law, we will cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations involving child safety.
