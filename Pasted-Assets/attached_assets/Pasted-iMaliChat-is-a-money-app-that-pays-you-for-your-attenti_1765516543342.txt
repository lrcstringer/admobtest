iMaliChat is a money app that pays you for your attention.

You earn tokens by watching short ads and answering tiny surveys – then you can:

build up a wallet balance

climb daily and weekly leaderboards

send and request value with friends

and spend on things like airtime (with more options coming over time)

No gambling, no guessing. Just fair rewards for time you already spend on your phone.

1. Earn tokens from short ads & surveys

Most of what you do in iMaliChat happens in the Earn area.

There you’ll see earn messages from different brands. Each one comes with:

a short ad (text, image, or video), and

a 1-question (or very short) multiple-choice survey.

How it works:

Tap an earn message to open it.

Watch/read the content:

For text or images, there’s a brief 3-second countdown so you actually see it.

For videos, you watch the full video.

Answer the quick survey question(s).

Every time you complete that flow, you earn tokens.
Tokens are your in-app money – they’re what you use in pots, Money Chat, and spending features.

2. Scores, streaks & leaderboards

Each time you earn, two things happen:

You get tokens → they go into your wallet.

You get score → this is used for leaderboards and prize pots.

The difference:

Tokens = guaranteed value you keep.

Score = how we rank you against other users.

If you come back regularly, you build a streak.
Streaks boost your score (not your tokens), helping you climb the leaderboard faster.

So the more consistently you use iMaliChat, the better your chances in the pots.

3. Daily & weekly pots (bonus rewards)

On top of your normal tokens, iMaliChat has two extra bonus pots:

a Daily Pot

a Weekly Pot

A small slice of the value from each completed ad + survey goes into these pots. At the end of each period:

The top users on the leaderboard share the pot.

In the Pots section you can always see:

How big today’s and this week’s pots are

How much time is left

Your current rank and score

You’ll always know:

“What’s in the pot, and how close am I to the top?”

4. Your wallet & history

All tokens you earn, win, or spend flow through your wallet.

Inside the wallet you’ll see:

Your current balance (tokens, and where relevant the Rand value)

A transaction history, including:

Earned from ads and surveys

Pot payouts

Money Chat sends/requests

Purchases (like airtime)

If you ever wonder “Where did that come from?” or “Where did it go?”, the wallet history is your answer.

5. Money Chat: move value between people

Money Chat lets you use your balance with people you trust.

In Money Chat you can:

See contacts who are already on iMaliChat

Open a thread with a friend

Send them an amount from your wallet

Request an amount from them

Every send or request shows up as:

a chat-style entry in your Money Chat thread, and

a line in your wallet history, so everything stays transparent.

If someone in your contacts isn’t on iMaliChat yet, you’ll see an Invite option instead of Send/Request.

6. Invite friends & earn together

You can invite friends directly from your phone contacts.

When you invite someone from inside iMaliChat:

We link their phone number to your account in the background (as long as you were the first to invite them).

When they sign up with that number and start using the app:

They get a starter bonus in tokens, and

You get a starter bonus in tokens, and

Over time, your assist score grows as they continue to earn.

In the My Referrals section you can see:

How many people you’ve invited

How many have joined

How many bonus tokens you’ve earned from referrals

Your assist score

The idea is to reward real, active referrals, not spammy invites.

7. Spending your tokens

As your tokens grow, you’ll be able to use them inside the app – starting with things like:

Airtime purchases

Over time, more ways to spend may be added (like data, electricity or partner rewards, depending on your market).

Every spend shows in your wallet history so you always know how your value is being used.
