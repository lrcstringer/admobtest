# Privacy Policy — iMaliChat

**Last updated: 2 April 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how iMali ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the iMaliChat mobile application ("the App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide
- **Identity:** Full name, display name, username
- **Contact:** Phone number (used for account creation and verification)
- **Profile:** Profile photo, gender, date of birth, province, city
- **Preferences:** Language preferences, interests

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Device:** Device identifier used for security and fraud prevention
- **Usage:** App activity, engagement history, last active time, online status
- **Survey & earn activity:** Survey responses, video watch history, token earnings
- **Token wallet:** Token balance, transaction history, cashout and airtime redemption records

### 1.3 Communications
- **Messages:** All peer-to-peer messages are end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) using the Signal Protocol (X3DH + Double Ratchet). We cannot read your messages. Message metadata (timestamps, participant IDs, read receipts) is stored on our servers.
- **Voice & video calls:** Conducted peer-to-peer via WebRTC. Call metadata is stored but call content is not recorded.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Verify your identity via SMS OTP
- Enable messaging, voice and video calls
- Administer the token earn and reward system
- Process airtime, data, and cashout redemptions via third-party providers
- Serve rewarded video advertisements through Google AdMob
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access
- Send push notifications about messages, earn opportunities, and rewards
- Improve the App and analyse usage patterns

---

## 3. Token Economy & Financial Data

iMaliChat operates a token-based rewards system:

- Users earn tokens by completing engagement tasks (watching videos, completing surveys)
- Tokens have no monetary value within the App and cannot be purchased
- Tokens may be redeemed for airtime, mobile data, or cash via third-party service providers
- All redemption transactions are processed by external providers; we share only the information necessary to fulfil your redemption request
- No fiat currency is stored or processed within the App

---

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

| Recipient | Purpose | Data Shared |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Google Firebase** | App infrastructure, authentication, database, storage, messaging | Account data, messages (encrypted), usage data |
| **Google AdMob** | Rewarded video advertisements | Ad engagement data, device identifiers |
| **Google Analytics (Firebase)** | App usage analytics | Anonymised usage events |
| **Airtime/data providers** | Fulfil token redemptions | Phone number, redemption amount |
| **Cashout providers** | Process token cashouts | Name, bank/payment details as required |
| **Brand partners** | Deliver earn opportunities | Survey responses, engagement completion |

Brand partners receive aggregated or anonymised engagement data unless you explicitly complete a task that requires sharing identifiable information.

---

## 5. Your Privacy Controls

You can control the following from Settings → Privacy:

- **Search visibility:** Who can find you via search (Everyone / Contacts only / Nobody)
- **Phone number visibility:** Who can see your phone number (Everyone / Contacts only / Nobody)
- **Profile photo visibility:** Who can see your photo (Everyone / Contacts only)
- **Last seen / online status:** Who can see when you were last active
- **Read receipts:** Show or hide when you have read messages
- **Group invitations:** Who can add you to groups and communities
- **Brand messages:** Control whether brands can message you

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account data:** Retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.
- **Messages:** Stored on your device in an encrypted local database. Server-side message metadata is retained for 30 days after delivery.
- **Transaction records:** Retained for 7 years for financial compliance purposes.
- **Survey responses:** Retained in anonymised form for analytics after 12 months.

---

## 7. Security

We implement the following security measures:

- End-to-end encryption (Signal Protocol) for all peer-to-peer messages
- AES-256-GCM encryption for reward codes and sensitive data
- Encrypted local database (SQLCipher)
- Biometric authentication and device binding
- Runtime Application Self-Protection (RASP) to detect tampering
- Device fingerprinting for fraud prevention

---
