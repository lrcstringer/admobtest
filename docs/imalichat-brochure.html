Feature Overview
🎯

Earn — Brand Engagement

Rewarded Ads Surveys Upload Tasks Audience Targeting

Brands create campaigns (Earn Threads) with individual tasks (Earn Opportunities). Users are matched by demographics, language, location, and interests — so you only see tasks that are relevant to you. Every completed task credits tokens instantly. A daily and weekly shared pot gives everyone a chance at bonus winnings.

💬

Chat — Secure Messaging

Signal Protocol E2EE Voice Notes Video Calls Communities

All messages are encrypted using the Signal Protocol (X3DH + Double Ratchet) — even we cannot read them. Send photos, videos, voice notes, and files. Make free voice and video calls over WebRTC. Create community group chats for stokvels, neighbourhoods, or teams.

🛒

Buy — Local Marketplace

Brand Storefronts Service Listings Group Buys VAS

Browse verified brand storefronts and independent service providers across 15+ categories — from home repairs to personal care to professional services. Pool buying power with others through Group Buys. Purchase airtime, data, and vouchers as Value-Added Services, all within the app.

💰

Wallet & Stokvel Savings

Token Wallet iStokvel Gooi-Gooi Group Goals

Your token wallet tracks every earning, spend, and transfer with a full double-entry ledger. Start or join a stokvel with configurable contribution cycles, rotating pot payouts (Gooi-Gooi), or shared savings goals. Redeem tokens for airtime, mobile data, or cash via trusted third-party partners.

🛡️

Enterprise-Grade Security — Built in from Day One

iMaliChat was architected with security as a first principle, not an afterthought. Every layer of the app is hardened against fraud, impersonation, and data leakage.

Hardware-backed device binding Biometric session lock Runtime RASP (freeRASP) E2EE Signal Protocol ECDSA keypairs Firebase App Check Double-entry ledger POPIA compliant
