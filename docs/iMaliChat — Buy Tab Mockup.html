Buy Tab — Design Notes

Layer 1 Featured Surface
  • Campaign card — brand-paid, token-action required. Swipeable carousel (dots shown). Vodacom example shown.
  • Collectible drop — dark card for visual contrast + urgency. Live countdown. Limited quantity. Shareable into Chat.
  • Trending strip — horizontal scroll. Live red dot. Community-generated signal (views, shares, chat-initiations). Geo-contextual header.
Layer 2 Utilities Hub
  • My Regulars — gold-tinted bar at top. Pre-populated from usage history. One-tap reorder chips. Dashed "+ Add" chip.
  • 4-column grid — all 7 categories shown. SA-specific: Funeral Policy, Stokvel, School Fees. Dashed "More" for future additions.
Layer 3 Intengiso Marketplace
  • Sub-market tabs — horizontal scroll. Active tab is green. All 5 markets accessible: Services, Second-Hand, Food, Sebenza, Hlangana.
  • Services cards — social proof ("3 of your contacts use her") as primary trust signal. Verified seed badge vs Favourite badge.
  • Hlangana card — progress bar with live count. Scarcity label in red. Token CTA button.

All layers are separated by an 8px grey divider band — a common SA super-app pattern (used in FNB App, Capitec, etc.) that groups content visually without heavy headers. The screen is designed to scroll continuously — Layer 1 is the hook, Layer 2 is the habit, Layer 3 is the destination.
