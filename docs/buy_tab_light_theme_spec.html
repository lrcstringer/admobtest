Design Rationale

Switch the Buy tab body from the app's dark gradient ( #142978 → #0C1124) to a light, neutral commerce surface.

The dark brand identity stays on the appBar and bottom nav bar — only the scrollable body changes. This approach:

  • Makes product cards pop. White cards on a light canvas have clear contrast without competing with chrome.
  • Reduces cognitive load for shopping. Users scan, compare, and decide faster on light surfaces.
  • Follows established commerce UX patterns. Every major commerce app (Takealot, Amazon, Shopify) uses light backgrounds for product browsing.
  • Preserves brand identity. Dark appBar + dark bottom nav frame the light content area, keeping the iMaliChat look.
BEFORE — Dark Gradient Body
Cards blend into dark chrome
AFTER — Light Commerce Surface
Cards pop with clear visual separation

Files NOT Modified

These files and elements are explicitly out of scope and must remain unchanged.

  • Featured carousel card rendering — cards keep their self-contained dark/branded gradient backgrounds
  • imali_app_bar.dart — stays dark (transparent over dark gradient)
  • Bottom nav bar — stays dark with brand styling
  • Any screen outside the Buy tab — Chat, Earn, Wallet, Profile tabs are unaffected
